# Seungho Woo

**Head of Future Strategy & Deputy Managing Editor, Seoul Economic Daily** — Seoul, Korea

I build AI for journalism, and the editorial rules that keep journalists in charge of it. We call the principle **AI HINT (Human-In-the-loop News Technology)**: machines do the work, people keep the judgment. *Let Humans Decide.*

## Key products

- **ATLAS** — a four-language (KO/EN/ZH/JA) knowledge graph of Korean corporate ownership, legislation, regulators and news, with the underlying data and formulas published so readers can re-check the numbers themselves.
- **AI LINK** ("Let News Flow") — the newsroom platform that joins our AI services into one workflow: AI NOVA, AI PRISM, AI WAVE, AI GLOBE, AI ECHO and AI LENS.
- **AI NOVA** — 20+ AI tools for reporters: copy-editing that points out what may be wrong without rewriting the story, drafting and research aids, and a rule that the journalist makes the final call.
- **AI PRISM** — briefs written for eight reader types from a single verified article.
- **AI WAVE / AI LENS** — video, and one story turned into article, webtoon, audio and video.
- **AI GLOBE** — the English edition, with every translated story linked back to its Korean original.
- **ECHO** — partner content produced and published by Seoul Economic Daily, then measured in AI answers.

## Data journalism

- **"The Power of Policy News"** (2026, with Sogang University) — 118,602 policy stories matched to the daily stock moves of 906 listed companies. Every figure was recomputed and audited before publication, and the results we decided not to publish are shown next to the ones we did.
- **ATLAS ownership paths** — direct and group-internal stakes of Korea's listed companies, the ownership rings between them (each with its step count and weakest stake), and public data files so the counting can be repeated.
